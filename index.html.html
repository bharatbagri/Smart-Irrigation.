About AgriSense

AgriSense is a smart irrigation concept that combines agriculture with Internet of Things technology to support better water management.

Sensors can measure soil moisture and send information to a controller. Based on configured conditions, the system can control irrigation.

  • Monitor soil moisture
  • Reduce unnecessary watering
  • Support efficient crop care
  • View sensor readings on a dashboard
